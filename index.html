The new discovery channel

Your next customer may ask AI before they ask you

Potential buyers are increasingly using AI platforms to decide:

  • which companies to consider
  • which providers appear trustworthy
  • which brands are most relevant
  • and which options should be avoided

Most companies do not know what these systems are saying about them.

They may be strong in the real market but almost invisible in AI-generated recommendations.

They may be mentioned after competitors, described weakly, associated with outdated information or excluded from consideration entirely.

Our diagnostic

AI Buyer Visibility

AI Buyer Visibility examines whether, when and how your company appears when potential customers ask leading AI platforms for recommendations.

It helps answer four basic questions:

  • Does your company appear?
  • How is it described?
  • Which competitors are recommended instead?
  • What may be limiting your visibility?
